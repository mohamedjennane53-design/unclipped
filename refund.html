Refund policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

If Unclipped Pro isn’t right for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of your purchase.

How to ask for a refund

  • Email unclippedsupport@gmail.com within 14 days of your purchase.
  • Send it from the email address you used to buy, or include your order number.
  • You don’t need to give a reason.

What happens next

The refund is processed through Polar, our merchant of record, to your original payment method. The time it takes to show up on your account depends on your bank. Once refunded, the license key is deactivated.

Questions

Write to unclippedsupport@gmail.com.

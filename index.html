How it works

Three steps, the same ones you see at the top of the app.

  1. 1

    Paste a stream link

    Copy the link of a Twitch or YouTube VOD and paste it. Unclipped reads the stream’s audio and its chat replay. You can also open a video file from your PC.

  2. 2

    Pick your moments

    You get 30 moments. Each one says why it came up and which signals spiked, with a thumbnail and a number that stays the same.

    Click a moment to preview it straight from the stream, trim its start and end, and keep or skip it. The keyboard works too: arrows to move, Space to play, X to keep.

  3. 3

    Export to your editor

    Export the moments you kept as a Premiere Pro or DaVinci Resolve timeline, or as MP4 files for CapCut and any other editor.

What it measures

Unclipped doesn’t guess what’s funny. It measures a few things you can check yourself, and shows you which ones spiked.

The streamer’s voice, not the game

The voice is separated from loud game sounds like explosions and gunfire, so a firefight on its own doesn’t count as a reaction.

  • Voice spike
  • Long reaction
How the chat reacts, in any language

Messages in caps, the same message spammed, how many different people type at once, and words that suddenly show up everywhere. There’s no word list to maintain, so it works in any language.

  • Chat in caps
  • Same message spammed
  • Many chatters
Compared to this stream only

Each signal is compared to the normal level of the stream you’re looking at, not to other streamers. A calm stream and a loud one are judged on their own terms.

Twitch clips that already exist

Each moment shows how many Twitch clips cover it. The counts are minimums: Twitch doesn’t always return every clip. Unclipped only sees Twitch clips. It doesn’t see TikTok, YouTube Shorts or other reposts.

  • No Twitch clips yet
  • 2 Twitch clips
  • 14 Twitch clips
A hook idea from Twitch

When a moment has Twitch clips, the title of the most viewed one is shown as a hook idea. No clips, no hook idea: nothing is made up.

It’s a measurement, not a verdict. You decide what makes a good clip.

Live mode

Follow a Twitch channel while it’s live. Unclipped watches the same voice and chat signals and saves each spike as you go.

  • Saved as an MP4 on your PC about 20 seconds after the peak.
  • Twitch clips counted as viewers make them, updated every minute.
  • A Windows notification when a new moment is saved.
  • Two channels at the same time.
  • Twitch ads are never recorded into your moments.

Live mode is part of Pro.

TikTok Rewards mode

Every moment is made 61 seconds or longer, because TikTok only pays for videos over one minute. The start moves closer to the peak so the hook comes fast.

FAQ

Is it allowed to clip streams?

Use Unclipped on your own streams, or on streams you have permission to clip, like an authorized clipping campaign. Follow the rules of Twitch, YouTube and the platforms you post on.

Does it see TikTok or YouTube Shorts?

No. Unclipped only sees clips made on Twitch. Reposts on TikTok, YouTube Shorts or anywhere else aren’t counted, and the Twitch counts are minimums.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not yet. Unclipped runs on Windows only for now.

Do I need a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time payment.

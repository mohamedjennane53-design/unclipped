Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Short version: Unclipped runs on your PC and doesn’t send your data to us. This website uses no cookies and no analytics.

The app

The analysis, your moments, your settings and your exports stay on your computer. We don’t run a server that receives them. To work, the app connects to these services directly from your PC:

  • Twitch, to read VOD information, the chat replay, live streams and the list of Twitch clips. Clip counts use your own Twitch account, which you connect in the app. The access token is stored on your PC, in the Windows Credential Manager.
  • YouTube, to read the information, audio and chat replay of the videos you paste.
  • GitHub, to download and update the open-source tools the app uses (yt-dlp and TwitchDownloaderCLI), and to check whether a new version of Unclipped is available.
  • Dodo Payments, the license platform, to activate and check your Pro license. It receives your license key and an identifier of your PC that doesn’t reveal who you are, so that a license works on 2 PCs at most.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. None of them sends your data to us through the app.

This website

This website doesn’t use cookies, trackers or analytics tools. Fonts and images are served from this website, not from third parties. It is hosted on GitHub Pages: like any web host, GitHub may log technical data such as your IP address for security. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

Payments

Purchases are processed by Dodo Payments, our merchant of record. Dodo Payments collects your payment details; we never see your card number. Dodo Payments shares with us what we need to deliver and support your license, such as your email address and country.

Emails

If you email us, we use your address and message only to answer you, or to tell you about the launch if you asked for it. We don’t sell or share them.
